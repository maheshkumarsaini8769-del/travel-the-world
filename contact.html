Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about traveling to Bali from India.

Do Indian passport holders get Visa on Arrival (VoA) in Bali? ▾
Yes! Indian passport holders can easily obtain a 30-day Visa on Arrival (VoA) at Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Denpasar, or pre-apply online via e-VoA. The fee is approximately IDR 500,000 (~₹2,650) and can be extended once for another 30 days.
Are pure vegetarian and Jain food options available during the tours? ▾
Absolutely! We partner with certified Indian restaurants in Seminyak, Kuta, Ubud, and Nusa Dua that serve authentic North and South Indian vegetarian and Jain cuisine. Simply notify us during booking or in the form above.
What happens if weather conditions disrupt our Mount Batur trek? ▾
Safety is our prime directive. In the rare event of extreme mountain weather or government volcanic advisories, we either reschedule your trek to the following morning free of charge, or provide a 100% full refund with zero cancellation deductions.
Can I pay in Indian Rupees (₹) via UPI or Net Banking? ▾
Yes! Our entire platform operates in Indian Rupees (`₹`). You can pay via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Indian Credit/Debit cards, or Net Banking with zero international forex conversion markups.
